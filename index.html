I'm a junior analyst at Clarity Campaign Labs working with a team of data scientists and analysts to provide customized strategies and solutions, including modeling, polling, and other tools, to progressive campaigns and organizations. I am experienced in data analytics and in using data to create actionable insights and solutions that actually drive change and solve problems.
I'm passionate about creating products that work for people, and I find that the most important work is often the work that happens after delivering a product. I'm interested in how documentation drives usage, how maintaining open relationships between developers and users allows for consistent reiteration and improvement, and how these processes can be improved with more attention and care.
My interests include using technology to improve the lives of working people and creating solutions using data that drive equitable access to tools that make our lives less difficult. In undergraduate studies, my research primarily organized around the implementation of data and LLMs as a tool to oppress, the political nature of data itself, and how to uphold equity in using statistical tools and data to explain the world that we live in.
